Essential Travel Tips:

  • Acclimatize properly to avoid altitude sickness.
  • Carry enough warm clothing and trekking gear, including a good pair of trekking boots.
  • Stay hydrated and bring energy snacks.
  • Plan your route and accommodations in advance.
  • Carry a first aid kit with medicines for altitude sickness, headaches, and minor injuries.
  • Check the weather forecast regularly and be prepared for sudden changes.
  • Travel with a reliable guide or in a group for safety.
  • Bring trekking poles to reduce strain on knees during steep climbs and descents.
  • Respect local culture and follow eco-friendly trekking practices.
