Top Spots Along Thorong La Trek

  • Muktinath Temple:

    This sacred temple is an important pilgrimage site for both Hindus and Buddhists. It is famous for its natural springs and stunning mountain views.

  • Kagbeni Village:

    Kagbeni is a traditional village known for its Tibetan culture and ancient monasteries. It offers trekkers a glimpse into local life along the Kali Gandaki River.

  • Thorung High Camp:

    Located at high altitude, Thorung High Camp serves as the final stop before crossing the Thorung La Pass. Trekkers rest here to acclimatize and prepare for the challenging ascent.

  • Tilicho Lake:

    Tilicho Lake is one of the highest lakes in the world, surrounded by breathtaking peaks. Its crystal-clear waters make it a rewarding side trek from the main route.
