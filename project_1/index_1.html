Welcome to My Travel Blog

I will take you through my experience on the Thorong La Pass, the biggest summit of the Annapurna Circuit in Nepal, which is an astounding 5,416 meters (17,769 ft). My blog is about my adventures when I treked through rocky mountainous areas and snow-white saddles and discovered beautiful scenery and incomparable experiences on the summit of the Himalayas.
I experienced sharp climbs, cold mornings and the greatness of thin air, understanding how to be acclimatized, how to walk at a slow pace and how to be persistent during the way. Every day I was to experience the colorful local culture, stunning sight of Annapurna and Dhaulagiri, and the feeling of great accomplishment as I passed through the mythical pass.
In this case, I will provide some tips on how to prepare to Thorong La, how to train the physical strength, how to have rest days, how to take the appropriate equipment and how to be safe in the unpredictable mountain conditions. When you are dreaming of conquer this amazing adventure, follow me and discover ups and downs of the outstanding Himalayan journey.
